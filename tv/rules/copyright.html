Copyright policy

This is a starter template written for Island Beats TV. It is not legal advice. Have it checked by a lawyer in Dominica before relying on it.

Only post what you own

Submit only videos you made yourself, or that you have written permission to use — including every beat, sample, song and clip in them.

Music and ECCO

Songwriters in Dominica and the Eastern Caribbean can register their works with ECCO (Eastern Caribbean Collective Organisation for Music Rights), which collects performance royalties. Using someone else's song without permission is not allowed, even if you credit them.

Copyright takedown request

If your work appears on Island Beats TV without permission, send this form. False claims may have legal consequences.

Counter-notice

If your video was removed and you believe it was a mistake or you have permission, reply to the removal email with your name, the video, and why you have the rights. Emmanuel may restore it after PLACEHOLDER: 10–14 days? unless the claimant shows they've taken further action.

Repeat infringers

Each valid takedown counts as a copyright strike. Three copyright strikes and the channel is removed.
