Community Guidelines

This is a starter template written for Island Beats TV. It is not legal advice. Have it checked by a lawyer in Dominica before relying on it.

Be creative, be respectful. Island Beats reviews every video before it goes up, and can remove any video later.

Spam, scams and misleading content

No fake giveaways, get-rich-quick schemes, phishing links, misleading titles or thumbnails, or posting the same thing over and over.

Nudity and sexual content

No pornography or sexual content. Artistic or educational content showing some skin may be age-restricted instead of removed.

Child safety

Zero tolerance for anything that sexualises, endangers or exploits a minor. Such content is removed, the account is closed, and it may be reported to the authorities.

Hate

No content attacking people for race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, sexual orientation, age or similar traits.

Harassment and bullying

No threats, targeted insults, sharing someone's private info, or encouraging others to pile on a person.

Violence and graphic content

No real violence shown to shock, no glorifying attacks, no threats. News or documentary context may be age-restricted.

Misinformation basics

No false claims that could cause real harm — for example fake health cures, false emergency or hurricane information, or lies about how to vote.

Dangerous acts

No challenges or stunts likely to cause serious injury, instructions for weapons or drugs, or encouraging self-harm. If you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or local emergency services.

Enforcement

Breaking these rules leads to removal and the strikes system.
