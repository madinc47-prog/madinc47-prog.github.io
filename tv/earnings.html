Creator earnings COMING SOON

Island Beats TV doesn't pay creators yet and collects no money. Nothing on this page is a promise. It explains what's being considered.

Options being considered

  • Supporter subscriptions — fans pay a small monthly amount to support the platform, shared with creators.
  • Tips — viewers send a one-off tip to a creator they like.
  • Sponsor ads — local businesses sponsor shelves or channels, like pinned ads on Island Beats Radio.
  • Revenue share by watch time — part of the money is split between creators based on how much their videos are watched.

What still has to be decided

  • Which payment provider works for Dominica and the Caribbean, and its fees.
  • Prices, and the creator / platform split.
  • Minimum payout amount, payout schedule and currency (XCD or USD).
  • Tax and identity checks for creators.

See the Partner program requirements for the eligibility rules being planned.
