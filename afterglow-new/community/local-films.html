How Local Films works

Filmmakers upload their film free to YouTube (unlisted is fine), Vimeo, Google Drive or the Internet Archive, then send us the link. Every submission is reviewed by a person before it appears here, and only films the filmmaker made or owns the rights to are accepted. Mature films stay hidden unless you switch them on. See something wrong? Open the film and press Report this film.
