How to host your film free

Afterglow doesn't store video files. Put your film on one of these free services first, then paste its link here.

YouTube (easiest)

  1. Go to YouTube Studio and press Create → Upload videos.
  2. Set visibility to Unlisted if you don't want it in YouTube search. Unlisted videos still play on Afterglow.
  3. Keep Allow embedding switched on (Studio → Content → video → Show more).
  4. Copy the share link (youtu.be/…).

Internet Archive (free, no ads)

  1. Make a free account at archive.org.
  2. Press Upload, add your video file, title and description, and pick a Creative Commons licence if you like.
  3. Copy the page link (archive.org/details/…).

Vimeo or Google Drive

  • Vimeo (free Basic plan): copy the video link. For unlisted videos use the full link that includes the private code.
  • Google Drive: right-click the video → Share → General access: Anyone with the link → copy link. Large files can take a while to process before they play.

What happens next

  • Your film goes to a private review queue. Nothing is public until it's approved.
  • We check that it plays, that the rating fits, and that it's yours to share.
  • You can ask us to take it down any time, and you keep all your rights.
