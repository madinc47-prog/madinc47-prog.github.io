How the rotation works

Titles come and go on a published schedule (now-showing.json). "New this week", "Leaving in 3 days" and "Last chance" tell you where each one is. Your 48-hour watch window starts the first time you press play. Honestly: these timers live only in this browser. They're a way of programming the channel, not copy protection, and they don't mean Afterglow owns the films. Every film stays free on its owner's own channel, and the owner can remove or region-lock it at any time.

Why these are on air

Every film here is free and legal to show: Creative Commons open movies (Blender Studio, CC BY 4.0), U.S. Government works (NASA, public domain in the U.S.), or full films the rights holder posted free on its own verified channel, embedded as-is. Afterglow does not re-host copyrighted studio films. Each card links to its source and license.

The Vintage Vault holds older films from the Internet Archive that the uploader marked public domain, played in the Archive's own embedded player.

The Action shelves follow the same rule: 80s classics posted free in full by their licensed distributor (Shout! Studios, which owns Shout! Factory), 2026 action films posted free by iQIYI on its official channel, and action shorts posted by the filmmakers themselves. Some distributor uploads are licensed only for certain countries; if YouTube blocks one where you are, the player says so.
