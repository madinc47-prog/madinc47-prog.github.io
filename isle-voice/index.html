Download preview

  1. Tapping Apply my voice saves one zip (song.zip) to your Downloads and opens the free Isle Voice notebook on Google Colab.
  2. In Colab: sign in with your Google account, tap Runtime → Run all, allow Google Drive, then pick the zip when asked.
  3. First time only: it trains your voice from your voice bank — about 45–120 minutes on Colab's free T4 GPU (plus ~5–10 min setup). The model is saved in your Google Drive (IsleVoice folder), so next time it skips straight to converting (~5–10 min).
  4. It converts the draft into your voice and downloads …_my_voice.wav. Come back here and import it.

Your audio only travels between this device and your own Colab session and Google Drive — no other file host. Colab's free GPUs aren't always available; see what to do if.

Apply my voice

Honest path: this browser app cannot make your voice sing by itself. Free open-source RVC does voice conversion on a free Colab GPU.

Includes voice bank WAVs, instrumental, guide melody, guide vocal takes, lyrics and step-by-step README.
  1. Train your voice model — open a free RVC or Applio Colab notebook, upload the voice_bank/ WAVs, and train (roughly 10–30 min of audio, 200–300 epochs is a common start on a free T4 GPU).
  2. Convert a sung vocal — upload your takes/ guide vocal (or any acapella you have rights to) and run inference with your model. Pick the pitch shift so it fits your range.
  3. Mix — put the converted vocal over instrumental.wav in a free DAW (Audacity, Cakewalk, BandLab, GarageBand).
  4. Release — only use voices you own or have permission to use.
