Speaker mode (no headphones)

The beat plays out loud so you can perform to it. Afterwards the take is cleaned on this device: the beat your mic picked up is subtracted, room noise is removed, and your clean vocal is mixed with the clean beat — like you wore headphones. Loud, distorted speaker playback can't be removed perfectly: keep the phone volume moderate.

How to capture video (free):
  • Desktop: use OBS Studio (free) — add a Window/Browser capture, crop to the frame, and record while the beat plays.
  • Phone: use the built-in screen recorder, then crop to 9:16 in your gallery editor.
  • For best audio, export the WAV from Vault and replace the audio track in your video editor (e.g. CapCut, DaVinci Resolve — both free).
  • Tap a frame to toggle fullscreen for a clean capture.
