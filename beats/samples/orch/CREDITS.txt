What was used (one dynamic layer, one round-robin per note):
  ostrings.mp3  Ensemble Strings  — Cello Section susvib, Viola Section susvib, Violin Section susVib
                                    (cello+viola / viola+violin sections mixed in unison where both exist)
  pizz.mp3      Pizzicato         — Cello Section pizzT, Viola Section pizz, Violin Section Pizz
  brass.mp3     Brass Section Stab — Trumpet stac, F Horn stac, Tenor Trombone stac (layered in unison)
  flute.mp3     Flute             — Flute susvib
  clarinet.mp3  Clarinet          — Clarinet susLong
  marimba.mp3   Marimba           — Percussion/Marimba (Outrigger, loud)
  orchhit.mp3   Orchestral Hit    — built for this app from string spiccato (cello/viola/violin), trumpet/horn/
                                    trombone staccato, a timpani hit and a crash cymbal, voiced root-fifth-octave

Processing (by Island Pin Beats): mono, onset-trimmed, fine-tuned to A=440, sustain levels flattened and
crossfade-looped, zones level-matched, packed one file per instrument (sync click at 50 ms), MP3 128 kbps.
Steel Pan and Kalimba are synthesized in the browser (no samples).
