How to · quick start
  1. Bring in a beat. Press ＋ Import and choose the Gunwalk beat, an audio file, or something from your Beats Vault. In the beat maker, Send to Studio bounces the current beat or pattern straight into a track.
  2. Record your voice. Press R on a track to arm it (it turns red), put the playhead where you want to start, then press ●. The other tracks play while you record. Press ■ to stop. You get a 1-bar count-in (change it in 🎧 Rec setup). Headphones give the cleanest takes. On the speaker, each take gets 🧹 Clean take automatically: the beat that leaked into the mic and the room noise are removed, and the raw take stays in the project (A/B to compare, Undo to go back). Arm another track for doubles and ad-libs; every take stacks on its own track.
  3. Edit clips. Drag a clip to move it (it snaps to beats when ⌗ Snap is on). Drag its left or right edge to trim, and the small squares at the top corners to fade in or out. ✂ Split cuts at the playhead; Copy, Paste and Duplicate work like a word processor.
  4. Fine-tune. Double-click a clip to open Waveform Edit. Drag to select, then Cut, Delete, Silence, Normalize, Amplify, Fade, Reverse or Trim. Edits never overwrite your recording: Undo steps back, and ⟲ Original restores the untouched take.
  5. Mix. Use each track's volume and pan, or the Mixer tab. FX adds EQ, compressor, noise gate, delay and reverb (try a preset). M mutes a track; S solos it.
  6. Loop and practise. Drag across the ruler to mark a loop and turn on ⟲ Loop. ♩ Click adds a metronome.
  7. Export. Export mixes everything to WAV or MP3. Projects save automatically in this browser; Save file downloads a copy you can open anywhere.

Keys: Space play/pause · R record · S split · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy/paste · Ctrl+D duplicate · Delete · Ctrl+Z undo · L loop · M click · Home to start.

Effects · Track

Signal flow: Gate → EQ → Compressor → Delay + Reverb → Pan → Fader. Changes are live while playing and are included in the export.
